# Voice

## Summary

Jordan sounds like a fractional GTM engineer who teaches by inversion and live builds: dry humor, Waymo/car-selfie asides, curse-friendly candor, and concrete examples (roofing credits, Segment startup credits, DoorDash menu deltas) before frameworks. Cadence is conversational “Howdy / Yo / What’s going on,” then a sharp thesis (“the list is the message,” “process over prompts,” “you are the guide, not the river”). Written Substack is structured (TL;DR, numbered lanes) but still mouthy (“everything shits the bed”). Spoken register mixes tactical empathy with roasting generic SDR personalization and AI-SDR theater. No X register in this distill — first-party pages did not list an official account.

## Patterns

- Inversion openers: work backwards from the message / closed-won / magic wand.
- Slogan stacks: “the list is the message”; “process over prompts”; “find, don’t filter”; “permissionless value prop.”
- Concrete micro-stories before abstract advice (pad thai pricing, roofing credit, Michelin vs Miller Lite).
- Roast of personalization theater (same hoodie / Notre Dame / “I saw you raised”).
- Guide-not-river / world’s-smartest-12-year-old framing for LLMs.
- Vertical vs horizontal: knowledge compounds vs fractures.
- Casual greetings: Howdy, Yo, What’s going on everyone.
- Self-deprecating operator bits (talks to AI more than humans; bad salesperson because inbound from content).
- Substack closes often note Claude-assisted draft + “Approved by Jordan.”
